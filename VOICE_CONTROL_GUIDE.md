# 🎤 Voice Control Guide - AI Video Player

## How to Use Voice Control

### Enable/Disable Voice Control
- Click the **🎤 ON/OFF** button in the video controls to toggle voice control
- By default, voice control is **enabled** when the page loads
- The button shows **🎤 ON** when active (green) and **🎤 OFF** when inactive (gray)

### Voice Commands

#### Basic Controls
Say any of these phrases after saying the wake word (optional):

**Playback Control:**
- `"play"` or `"start"` or `"resume"` - Play the video
- `"pause"` or `"hold"` - Pause the video
- `"stop"` or `"reset"` - Stop and reset video to beginning

**Volume Control:**
- `"volume up"` or `"louder"` or `"turn up"` or `"increase"` - Increase volume by 10%
- `"volume down"` or `"quieter"` or `"turn down"` or `"less volume"` - Decrease volume by 10%
- `"mute"` or `"silent"` - Mute the video
- `"unmute"` or `"sound on"` or `"sound"` - Unmute the video

**Playback Speed:**
- `"speed up"` or `"faster"` - Increase playback speed by 0.25x
- `"speed down"` or `"slower"` - Decrease playback speed by 0.25x

**Navigation:**
- `"forward"` or `"skip"` or `"10 seconds"` or `"skip ahead"` - Skip ahead 10 seconds
- `"rewind"` or `"back"` or `"go back"` or `"10 seconds back"` - Rewind 10 seconds

**Help:**
- `"help"` or `"commands"` - Show available commands

### Wake Words (Optional)
You can use these wake words to trigger commands:
- **"Hey"** - e.g., "Hey play", "Hey pause"
- **"OK"** - e.g., "OK play", "OK volume up"
- **"Hey video"** - e.g., "Hey video play"
- **"OK video"** - e.g., "OK video pause"

**Note:** Wake words are optional! You can also say commands directly without them.

### Visual Feedback
- 🎤 **Listening** (Blue) - Voice control is actively listening
- ⚡ **Processing** (Orange) - Command is being processed
- ✓ **Success** (Green) - Command executed successfully
- ℹ **Info** (Blue) - Information or error message

### Requirements
- **Modern Browser:** Chrome, Edge, Opera, or any browser supporting Web Speech API
- **Microphone:** Ensure microphone is enabled and connected
- **Internet:** Voice recognition requires internet connection
- **Audio Input Permission:** Grant microphone access when prompted

### Troubleshooting

**Voice control not working?**
1. Check if microphone is enabled and has permission
2. Make sure internet connection is active
3. Try clicking the **🎤 ON** button to restart voice control
4. Check browser console for errors (F12 → Console tab)

**Commands not recognized?**
1. Speak clearly and at normal volume
2. Make sure there's not too much background noise
3. Try using specific command keywords (e.g., "play" instead of "start")

**Microphone permission denied?**
1. Check browser settings to allow microphone access
2. For Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Microphone
3. Reload the page and grant permission

### Supported Browsers
- ✅ Google Chrome (57+)
- ✅ Microsoft Edge (79+)
- ✅ Opera (44+)
- ✅ Safari (14.1+) - Limited support
- ❌ Internet Explorer - Not supported

### Tips for Best Experience
- Use a good quality microphone
- Minimize background noise
- Speak naturally and clearly
- Use short command phrases
- Say the wake word clearly if using one
- Keep the video player page active (in focus)
